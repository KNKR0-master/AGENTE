## Objetivo do piloto

Validar se o Google Flow consegue gerar ou editar assets de video curtos para TikTok Shop com qualidade suficiente para teste, sem claims medicos e com registro rastreavel.

## Regras de compliance

Nao usar:

- cura;
- tratamento;
- ansiedade;
- sono;
- dor;
- caibra;
- emagrecimento;
- resultado garantido;
- antes/depois fisiologico;
- linguagem medica ou diagnostica.

Pode usar:

- rotina;
- organizacao;
- leitura de rotulo;
- comparacao de oferta;
- explicacao neutra dos quatro tipos de magnesio;
- compra consciente;
- unboxing.

## Hipoteses criativas

| ID | Hipotese | O que queremos aprender |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Leitura de rotulo | Se conteudo educativo neutro passa confianca sem claim sensivel. |
| H2 | Oferta por unidade | Se a promocao de 1, 2 ou 3 unidades e clara e gera interesse. |
| H3 | Rotina de organizacao | Se o produto funciona visualmente como item de rotina/autocuidado. |

## Prompt 1 - leitura de rotulo

```text
Create a short vertical TikTok Shop UGC-style video for a Brazilian supplement product called Tetra Magnesio, 60 capsules.
Show the bottle/package clearly in the first seconds on a clean kitchen or desk surface.
The concept is: "before buying a supplement online, check the label first".
Scene 1: close-up of the product on the table, hand turning it gently.
Scene 2: simple visual focus on the label and the four magnesium types, without medical claims.
Scene 3: product next to a notebook checklist with the words "rotulo", "ingredientes", "quantidade", "oferta".
Do not mention cure, sleep, anxiety, pain, cramps, weight loss, metabolism claims, or guaranteed results.
Keep it realistic, natural, clean, and suitable for a Brazilian TikTok Shop affiliate video.
Duration: 8 to 10 seconds.
```

Edicao/variacao:

```text
Make the video more natural and less like an advertisement.
Add a simple handheld UGC feel, keep the product readable, and make the first 2 seconds stronger.
Do not add health promises or medical claims.
```

## Prompt 2 - oferta por unidade

```text
Create a short vertical TikTok Shop product video for Tetra Magnesio, 60 capsules, focused on comparing purchase options.
Use a clean desk setup with one bottle, then two bottles, then three bottles arranged side by side.
Scene 1: show one unit clearly with a simple price tag style overlay placeholder.
Scene 2: show two units together as a "stock up" option.
Scene 3: show three units together and a simple checklist: "preco por unidade", "frete", "avaliacoes".
Avoid medical claims, cure promises, before/after, or exaggerated health benefits.
Make it feel like a practical shopping tip, not a medical recommendation.
Duration: 8 to 10 seconds.
```

Edicao/variacao:

```text
Improve clarity of the three purchase options.
Make the product packaging the visual focus.
Remove any text that sounds like a health promise.
Keep it suitable for TikTok Shop affiliate content in Brazil.
```

## Prompt 3 - rotina de autocuidado

```text
Create a realistic vertical UGC-style video showing Tetra Magnesio as part of a simple daily organization routine.
Scene 1: morning desk or kitchen counter with water glass, calendar, and the product package visible.
Scene 2: hand placing the product near a small routine checklist.
Scene 3: close-up of the product next to the checklist with neutral text: "organize sua rotina" and "confira o rotulo".
Do not imply the supplement causes energy, sleep improvement, pain relief, anxiety relief, weight loss, or medical benefits.
Keep the tone calm, practical, natural, and compliant.
Duration: 8 to 10 seconds.
```

Edicao/variacao:

```text
Make the routine feel more Brazilian and less generic.
Keep the product clearly visible.
Avoid all health result claims.
Make the clip more suitable as b-roll for a TikTok Shop affiliate review.
```
